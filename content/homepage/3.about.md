#story

## Hi, I'm Shannon

A few years ago at 52, after 30+ years in international business, I had achieved everything that looked impressive on paper. But I came home to profound loneliness.

So I bought a single bed, convinced I was destined to be alone.

Then my coach asked: "What would a date with you look like?"
I cried. Because honestly? A date with me sounded awful.

But that question changed everything. I decided to take myself on a real date - to my favorite beach restaurant. I savored every bite, walked barefoot on the sand, then danced in my living room to Latin music. It was the best date of my life.

One year later, I met my soulmate. We're now happily married.

Here's what I learned:

> To create your ideal love story, you must first fall in love with your greatest love - the one inside you.

Now I guide other successful women through this same transformation. Because if I can go from buying a single bed in resignation to finding soulmate love by learning to date myself first, so can you.

Whether you want to **live your best life** on your own or be open to the next chapter of your life, including a fulfilling love relationship, both are possible when you *have the foundation of reconnecting with yourself - and fully loving the greatest love within you.*

> Your greatest love truly lives within you.
