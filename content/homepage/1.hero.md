Start by dating yourself. Remember who you are. Reconnect with the greatest love inside you.  And rediscover what you love. 

*I went from a single bed to soulmate love in a year. If I can, you can too!*

Sometimes the next chapter begins by giving yourself space - to reconnect with who you are and hear what you need and desire.

Life can get busy, complicated, demanding, and full of expectations. 

Somewhere in the middle of caring for responsibilities, relationships, work, family, and everything else, you may realize that you have become disconnected from one very important person:   **You**.

Maybe you know something needs to change, but you are not sure what. Maybe you are craving more joy, peace, confidence, connection, or clarity. Maybe you simply want space to think about what you want now. 

You do not have to have everything figured out before you begin. You only have to be willing to listen to yourself again. 

**Your greatest love lives within you!**

With that as your foundation, you can live your best life, have more fulfilling relationships, or potentially have an amazing love relationship (even if that currently seems impossible).
