
### Reconnect, Rediscover, Remember who you are

Join our intimate group of sacred soul sisters, 
called a PJ Circle (Peace & Joy Circle). 

PJ Circles are intimate biweekly gatherings where successful women come home to themselves. We strip away the 'shoulds' and remember our divine essence in sacred community.
This is perfect if you're thinking: "I don't remember how to be just by myself".
